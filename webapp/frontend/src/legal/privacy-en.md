# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 May 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains what information this library collects, why, and what your rights are. It applies to everyone who visits or uses this library, including unregistered visitors.

## 1. Who we are

This library is operated by Tigran Aivazian, based in the United Kingdom. You can reach the administrator at **{{contact_email}}**.

## 2. What information we collect

### On our server

When you visit any page in this library, the following is automatically recorded:

- **IP address** — the network address your device uses to reach us.
- **Browser identifier** (User-Agent) — the kind of browser and operating system you use.
- **The pages you visit** — directories you browse, books you open, searches you make.
- **Actions you take in the library** — for example, organizing books into playlists (creating or deleting a playlist, adding or removing items, or changing whether it is private or shared), and similar interactions with library features.
- **Login and registration events** — the time you sign in, whether successfully or not.
- **Administrative actions** (only if you have administrator privileges) — for example, recommending a book or removing it from the Recommended list.

When you take such an action as an administrator, your display name (the **Real name** field on your profile — never your email address) is shown to other users, including unregistered visitors, alongside the affected book, so it is transparent who curates content. Administrators who leave the Real name field blank are not identified by name.

If you are signed in, these events are linked to your account. If you visit without an account, they are linked only to your IP address.

### On your device

After you sign in, we store an **authentication cookie** in your browser so you remain signed in across visits. This cookie is strictly necessary for the library to recognize you.

We also store a few **user preferences** in your browser's local storage: theme (light/dark), interface language, view mode (grid/list), reader font size and family, annotation visibility, and thumbnail size. These preferences stay on your device, are not sent to our servers, and are not used for tracking.

We do **not** use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, fingerprinting, or any third-party scripts. No consent banner is required because no client-side storage is used for tracking.

## 3. Why we collect this

We use the information collected to:

- Understand how the library is being used and which materials are most read.
- Detect and prevent abuse, scraping, or unauthorized access.
- Operate and improve the library.

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for their own purposes.

## 4. Legal basis

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), our legal basis for processing this information is **legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f)) — specifically, our interest in operating, securing, and improving the library. Where you have an account, **contract** (Article 6(1)(b)) is the basis for any processing required to provide your account.

You may object to processing on legitimate-interest grounds at any time — see Section 8.

## 5. Geolocation

Your IP address is converted into an approximate country and city using an offline geolocation database (MaxMind GeoLite2-City), which runs entirely on our server. **Your IP address is never sent to any external service** for this purpose.

## 6. How long we keep your data

- **Usage events** (IP, page visits, searches, book opens): **24 months**, then automatically deleted.
- **Account information** (email, real name, avatar, ratings, comments, annotations): kept until you delete your account or remove the content.
- **Backups**: our database is backed up nightly and kept for **14 days** before being deleted.
- **Server logs**: standard HTTP logs are written to systemd journal and retained per the operating system's default journal retention (typically 1 month).

## 7. Third parties

The library is fully self-hosted. We use:

- **MaxMind GeoLite2** — offline database, no data sent to MaxMind during use.
- **An SMTP provider** — receives recipient email addresses solely for delivering transactional email (registration approvals, notification digests).

We use no analytics services, no advertising networks, no social media plugins.

## 8. Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Correct** inaccurate or incomplete data.
- **Delete** your data (the "right to be forgotten").
- **Restrict** how we process your data.
- **Object** to our processing on legitimate-interest grounds.
- **Data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

**If you have an account**, you can view and export your usage history at [your activity page](#/account/activity) and delete it from the same page. To delete your account entirely, email the administrator.

**If you visit without an account**, we cannot reliably verify your identity, so erasure requests are best-effort. Email the administrator with the approximate dates and an IP address you control, and we will delete matching events.

We respond to all requests within **30 days**.

## 9. Children

This library is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact the administrator.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent change. Material changes will be announced on the library home page and emailed to registered users.

## 11. Complaints

If you believe we have mishandled your personal data, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority in your country.
